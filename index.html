OUR STORY

A Seattle legacy reborn, block by block.

From Turf Restaurant in 1936 to Ludi's Diner on 2nd Ave, owner Gregorio "Uncle Greg" Garcia and his family have spent nearly nine decades feeding downtown Seattle worker bees, market vendors, and hungry visitors.

After closing in 2019 due to redevelopment, Ludi's proudly reopened at 120 Stewart Street in late 2023 — bringing back our legendary purple Ube pancakes, garlic-laden silog plates, and warm family hospitality right next to Pike Place Market.
